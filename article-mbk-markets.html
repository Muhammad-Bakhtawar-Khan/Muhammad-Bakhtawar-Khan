MBK Markets: exploring a clearer view of market data

A cryptocurrency market dashboard built to bring a broad coin catalogue, live market information and technical charts into one interface.

MBK Markets presents information for more than 10,000 cryptocurrency coins, helping visitors browse a wide range of digital assets from one place. The interface is designed to make market information easier to scan, compare and explore instead of overwhelming visitors with an unstructured list of numbers.

Interactive charts include candlestick-style views that help users inspect price movements and market trends over time. Live values and chart details depend on the connected data source and its update status, so users should check the displayed timeframe and source before relying on a figure.

The project explores data presentation, responsive layout and chart-driven market analysis. It is an informational tool, not financial advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any asset.
