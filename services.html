THE MBK SERVICE APPROACH / GUIDE

How our digital services work together

Good digital work is more than a good-looking screen. A business needs a clear message, an experience customers can understand, reliable technical implementation and a practical way to reach the right audience. MBK’s service approach brings those parts together without pretending that one solution fits every business.

From brand identity to a working website

Visual design helps people recognize a brand and understand its offer. Website design turns that message into page layouts and user journeys, while website development makes those layouts responsive and functional. Depending on the brief, a project may include a landing page, a personal portfolio, a business website, a product catalogue or a web application with interactive features.

Before building, we identify the key audience, main action, required pages, content needs and technical constraints. This helps keep the project focused and avoids adding features that do not serve the user.

APIs, apps and connected workflows

APIs let software services exchange data. API integration can connect a website to a supported data provider, form, business tool or other service. Web applications and dashboards can then present the information in a clearer workflow. Mobile or cross-platform application work depends on the required platform, complexity and delivery plan, so scope is confirmed case by case.

WhatsApp bots and AI automation

Messaging bots and workflow automation can help answer common questions, capture leads, send updates or route requests. AI features may help draft responses, search approved information or assist with repetitive tasks. The right design includes clear limits, human hand-off when needed, privacy considerations and the rules of the services being integrated. Automation should save time without misleading customers or sending unwanted messages.

SEO, content and advertising creative

Search visibility begins with useful pages, descriptive titles, logical navigation, crawlable links and content that answers real questions. Technical SEO can support discovery through canonical URLs, XML sitemaps, image descriptions and structured data where suitable. No ethical SEO provider can guarantee a particular ranking or Google sitelinks. Social and advertising creatives support campaigns, but campaign performance also depends on audience, offer, budget, landing page and ongoing testing.

Security, quality and responsible delivery

Code analysis and security review must be authorized and limited to agreed systems. A responsible delivery process considers mobile layouts, broken links, form behavior, performance basics, accessibility, secrets and configuration. Deployment, hosting and maintenance requirements are discussed in advance; domain registration, paid APIs, advertising spend and other third-party fees are not assumed to be included unless the proposal says so.

What you receive

Depending on the selected service, deliverables may include design files, responsive pages, source code, integration setup, content, metadata, documentation, deployment assistance or a handover guide. The final list is written into the project scope so both sides understand what is included and what is not.
