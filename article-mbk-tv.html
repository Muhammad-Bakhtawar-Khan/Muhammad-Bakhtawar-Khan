MBK TV: thinking about a digital media destination

An online TV experience for discovering and watching available live channels and streaming content in one place.

MBK TV brings together a digital media interface where visitors can explore available channels, including ARY, Geo, Green TV and Dawat-e-Islami. The aim is to make live-channel discovery straightforward, with a clear route from browsing to viewing.

The platform is designed around online TV and streaming access. Availability and playback depend on the stream, region, provider and current integration. Any third-party services or subscription content, including Netflix, remain subject to their own official access requirements and licensing.

This project explores media discovery, readable navigation and a practical viewing experience across screen sizes. Channel availability may change over time.
