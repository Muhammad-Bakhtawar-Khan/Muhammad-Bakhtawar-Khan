MBK TV: thinking about a digital media destination

A media concept where clarity, navigation and a strong editorial structure can make content easier to discover.

MBK TV is one of the digital projects in the MBK collection. The idea behind a media platform is simple: visitors should be able to understand what the platform covers, discover relevant content and move between sections naturally.

Media websites need more than a logo and a feed. They benefit from consistent categories, readable headlines, thoughtful page speed and metadata that describes each page accurately. If content is updated regularly, a sitemap and clear internal links also help search engines understand the site's structure.

The project is an ongoing web experiment, and its exact features may change. I see it as a chance to explore how a digital media experience can balance visual identity with practical usability.
