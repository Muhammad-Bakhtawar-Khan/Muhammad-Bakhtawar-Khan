01 PROFILE

A PERSONAL INTRODUCTION

Bakhtawar Khan: learning, building and sharing in public

A digital portfolio is more than a list of links. It can be a record of curiosity, progress and the work still ahead.

I’m Bakhtawar Khan, also known online as MBK. I’m interested in web development, practical AI tools, APIs, automation and the craft of making digital experiences feel clear and useful. My approach is to learn through small projects: plan a simple idea, build an interface, test how it behaves and keep improving it.

This website brings my projects, writing, visual work and online profiles together in one place. It is a work in progress by design. I want it to show not only finished links, but also the process behind them: what I’m exploring, what I’ve learned and what I want to make better next.

My focus is on steady progress rather than pretending to know everything. I value practical learning, honest descriptions and the kind of collaboration where a clear problem leads to a useful result.

02 BRAND

THE DIGITAL STUDIO IDEA

MBK Global: a home for creative digital work

A brand direction that brings web experiences, design thinking and technology experiments under one identity.

MBK Global represents the broader digital side of my work: websites, online experiences, creative concepts and experiments with emerging tools. The intention is to keep the brand flexible enough to explore different ideas while keeping one consistent standard—clear communication and thoughtful execution.

Good digital work is not only about adding effects or making a page look impressive. It should help people understand what is offered, find what they need and take the next step without friction. That means content hierarchy, accessibility, performance and responsive behaviour matter just as much as visual style.

As the projects grow, MBK Global can serve as a shared home for selected work and experiments. The brand is evolving alongside my skills, and this journal will help document that process.

03 MEDIA

CONTENT & DISCOVERY

MBK TV: thinking about a digital media destination

A media concept where clarity, navigation and a strong editorial structure can make content easier to discover.

MBK TV is one of the digital projects in the MBK collection. The idea behind a media platform is simple: visitors should be able to understand what the platform covers, discover relevant content and move between sections naturally.

Media websites need more than a logo and a feed. They benefit from consistent categories, readable headlines, thoughtful page speed and metadata that describes each page accurately. If content is updated regularly, a sitemap and clear internal links also help search engines understand the site's structure.

The project is an ongoing web experiment, and its exact features may change. I see it as a chance to explore how a digital media experience can balance visual identity with practical usability.

04 DATA UI

MAKING INFORMATION LEGIBLE

MBK Markets: exploring a clearer view of market data

A market interface is a design challenge: show useful information without overwhelming the person reading it.

MBK Markets explores the presentation of market-related information through a web interface. Financial data can be dense, and a useful interface needs a strong hierarchy: the most important values should be easy to scan, supporting details should be available when needed and the layout should remain usable on smaller screens.

Charts and price figures also need context. Users should be able to identify what a number represents, the time period it covers and whether the data is current. Visual polish is valuable, but it should never imply that information is live or authoritative unless the underlying data source supports that claim.

This project is a learning space for data presentation, responsive layout and the details that make information easier to understand. It is not financial advice.

05 WEB TOOLS

SMALL UTILITIES, REAL LESSONS

MBK Downloader and Text to Handwriting: focused tools for focused tasks

A useful tool should make one job feel straightforward, not turn a simple task into a complicated workflow.

MBK Downloader and Text to Handwriting explore two different utility ideas. A downloader interface should explain what input is expected, present available options clearly and communicate errors without confusing the visitor. It also needs to respect the terms and rights that apply to the content being handled.

A handwriting-style tool is a creative experience. Its value comes from giving typed text a more personal visual treatment while keeping the workflow simple: enter text, adjust the output and decide what to do next. Readability and export quality matter more than decorative complexity.

Working on small utilities is a useful way to practice validation, interface states, responsive behaviour and API integration. It also reinforces an important product lesson: the best interface is often the one that removes unnecessary steps.

06 LEARNING

CONTENT WITH CARE

Noor-e-Quran: creating a calm digital learning experience

A faith-oriented website benefits from simplicity, respect and a clear path to the content visitors came to find.

Noor-e-Quran is part of the MBK project collection. A website in this space should put the learning experience first: readable typography, comfortable spacing, thoughtful navigation and a calm visual style can help visitors focus on the content rather than the interface.

Accuracy and trust are especially important for religious material. Sources should be clearly identified, content should be handled respectfully and any translations or explanatory text should be reviewed carefully. Accessibility on mobile devices also matters, because many visitors use a phone as their primary screen.

The project is an opportunity to think about how design choices can support a meaningful subject without distracting from it.

07 PROCESS

THE WORK NEVER REALLY STOPS

What comes next for the MBK portfolio

A professional portfolio grows through accurate details, regular maintenance and real evidence of work.

The next stage is not simply to add more pages. It is to make every page more useful: keep project links working, add screenshots and case studies where available, explain the problem each project addresses and document the decisions made along the way.

For the website itself, that means keeping navigation consistent, making images accessible, checking page speed, adding descriptive metadata and maintaining an accurate sitemap. For the writing, it means publishing real updates when there is something meaningful to share rather than filling the site with empty announcements.

This portfolio should continue to reflect where I am today while leaving room for what I learn next. The goal is a clear, honest picture of the person, projects and ideas behind MBK.
