Web Development

Web development covers planning, building and maintaining websites and web interfaces. A project can include responsive page layouts, accessible forms, content structure, metadata and deployment preparation. The right scope depends on goals, content readiness, integrations, hosting and maintenance needs.

Possible scope

  • Business and portfolio websites
  • Landing pages and project pages
  • Responsive layouts and navigation
  • Contact forms and third-party integrations
  • Technical handover and deployment guidance

How a project starts

  1. Project goals and audience
  2. Required pages and content
  3. Brand assets and design references

Cost and timing

Cost and delivery timing depend on scope, content readiness, integrations, third-party services, testing and revision needs. A quotation should be provided only after the requirements are reviewed. No search ranking, traffic, conversion or security outcome is guaranteed.
