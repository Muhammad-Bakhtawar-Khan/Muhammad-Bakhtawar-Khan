MBK Downloader and Text to Handwriting: focused tools for focused tasks

A multi-platform video downloader that turns a supported video link into a simple, focused download workflow.

MBK Video Downloader is built to work with supported platforms such as YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok, alongside other sources supported by its current downloader service. The goal is to let visitors paste a video link and use the available download formats or quality options without navigating a complicated interface.

Platform support, available formats and download quality can vary with the source platform and the connected service. Users should only download content they own or are authorized to save, and follow each platform's terms and applicable copyright rules.

The related Text to Handwriting project is a separate creative utility that transforms typed text into a handwriting-inspired presentation. Together, these tools explore simple inputs, clear options, API integration and useful interface states.
