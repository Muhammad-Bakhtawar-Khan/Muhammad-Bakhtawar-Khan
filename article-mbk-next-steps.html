07 PROCESS

THE WORK NEVER REALLY STOPS

What comes next for the MBK portfolio

A professional portfolio grows through accurate details, regular maintenance and real evidence of work.

The next stage is not simply to add more pages. It is to make every page more useful: keep project links working, add screenshots and case studies where available, explain the problem each project addresses and document the decisions made along the way.

For the website itself, that means keeping navigation consistent, making images accessible, checking page speed, adding descriptive metadata and maintaining an accurate sitemap. For the writing, it means publishing real updates when there is something meaningful to share rather than filling the site with empty announcements.

This portfolio should continue to reflect where I am today while leaving room for what I learn next. The goal is a clear, honest picture of the person, projects and ideas behind MBK.
