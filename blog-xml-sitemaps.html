XML Sitemaps for Small Websites

What a sitemap should include and why it should list canonical, indexable URLs.

Why this topic matters

What a sitemap should include and why it should list canonical, indexable URLs. This guide is part of the learning notes and digital project journal associated with Muhammad Bakhtawar Khan (MBK) and MBK Global.

Practical points to consider

Start with the real need, explain the intended outcome, test assumptions and document limitations. Prefer accurate information, accessible interfaces and security-conscious decisions. Review the result on mobile and desktop, and ask users for specific feedback where appropriate.

  • Keep information clear, accurate and relevant to the page.
  • Use descriptive headings and links that explain where they lead.
  • Protect private information and use tools only with appropriate authorization.
  • Review performance, accessibility and usability before publishing changes.
