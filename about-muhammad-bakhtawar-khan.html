Muhammad Bakhtawar Khan (MBK)

Muhammad Bakhtawar Khan, also known as MBK, is a digital technology professional and the founder of MBK Global. His interests and project work include web development, AI-related solutions, API integration, cybersecurity, SEO, AEO, GEO and digital technology.
